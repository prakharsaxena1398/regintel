Title: GST Circular No. 178/2022 - Mandatory E-Invoicing for Taxpayers above the Aggregate Turnover Threshold

Version: Circular No. 178/2022, issued on 03 August 2022 by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), in suppression of Circular No. 163/2019 to the extent of inconsistency.

1. Scope of mandatory e-invoicing
Every registered person whose aggregate turnover in any financial year from 2017-18 onwards exceeds Rs 10 crore shall prepare invoices in respect of supplies of goods or services or both in the standardised e-invoice format and shall report the same to the Invoice Registration Portal within the time limit specified in sub-rule (4) of rule 48 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017. The requirement shall apply irrespective of the nature of the supply, and a registered person shall not issue a tax invoice in any other format once the requirement becomes applicable to him.

2. Registration on the portal
A registered person to whom this circular applies shall obtain enrolment on the e-invoicing portal before generating any e-invoice, and shall map each of his premises and each of his document series to the portal at the time of enrolment. The registered person shall sign the e-invoice with a digital signature issued by a certifying authority licensed under the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the signed invoice shall carry a valid Invoice Reference Number (IRN).

3. Generation and validity of the IRN
The Invoice Reference Number generated by the portal shall remain valid for the life cycle of the invoice, and a registered person shall not generate a fresh IRN for the same invoice.

4. Consequences of non-compliance
Where an invoice is issued without following the procedure in this circular, such invoice shall not be treated as a valid tax invoice, and the registered person shall be liable to penalty under section 122 of the Act.
