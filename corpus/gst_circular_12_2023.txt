Title: GST Circular No. 12/2023 - Clarifications on Input Tax Credit under Section 16(2)(b)

Version: Circular No. 12/2023, issued on 17 July 2023 by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), Government of India, Ministry of Finance.

1. Purpose
This circular clarifies the conditions for availing input tax credit (ITC) by registered persons under clause (b) of sub-section (2) of section 16 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017. Trade representations have been received regarding the sequence in which credit may be availed where the supplier has not discharged his tax liability. The Board has examined the matter and issues the following clarifications, which apply to all registered persons claiming ITC on inward supplies of goods or services or both.

2. Conditions for availing credit
A registered person shall avail input tax credit on any invoice only after the supplier concerned has deposited the tax with the government, either in cash or by utilising input tax credit available in his electronic credit ledger. The recipient shall verify that the invoice appears in his statement of inward supplies furnished in FORM GSTR-2B before taking credit in respect of that invoice. Where the tax shown in the invoice has not been paid by the supplier, the recipient shall first approach the supplier for payment of tax, and only thereafter shall the recipient remit the tax along with interest under section 50, if payment is not made by the supplier within forty-five days of the issue of the invoice.

3. Relaxation for smaller taxpayers
A registered person whose aggregate turnover in the preceding financial year does not exceed Rs 5 crore may avail the credit in respect of any number of monthly statements in FORM GSTR-3B, and the restriction on the number of statements shall not apply to such person.

4. Verification and records
Every registered person claiming credit shall maintain records of the correspondence with the supplier, and any contradiction between this circular and the provisions of the Act shall be resolved in favour of the Act.
