Title: FSSAI Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) Regulations, 2018 - Excerpt on Packaging Materials

Version: Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) Regulations, 2018, notified on 12 October 2018 by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India, effective from 01 July 2019.

1. General requirements for packaging materials
Every food business operator shall use only packaging materials that comply with the Indian Standards specified in the Schedule, and the packaging material shall be suitable for the type of food packed. Packaging materials shall not transfer any constituent to the food in quantities that may endanger human health or bring about a change in the composition of the food or a deterioration of its flavour and odour. Where a food business operator changes the packaging material used for a product, the operator shall carry out a migration test for the new material before the product is released for sale.

2. Plastic materials and contact surfaces
Plastic materials intended to come into contact with food shall conform to the specification for overall migration limits notified by the Authority, and the manufacturer shall obtain a certificate of conformity from a laboratory recognised by the Authority for every batch of food contact plastic. A food business operator shall not use recycled plastics for packaging food products unless the recycling process has been approved by the Authority, and the operator shall maintain records of the supplier approval for each consignment of recycled material.

3. Multipack and general packaging
Where a package consists of an outer packaging containing several units of the same food, the outer packaging shall carry a declaration of the number of units and the net quantity of each unit.

4. Inspection and records
Designated officers shall inspect the premises of food business operators for compliance, and every operator shall preserve records of packaging material procurement and test reports for not less than three years.
